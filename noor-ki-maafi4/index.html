Chapter 1

The Shart I Refused

Tumne ek shart rakhi thi. Maine mana kar diya. Tumne bina kuch kahe call kaat di. Uske baad na tumne mauka diya, na maine jaldi se samjha.

"Tumhara gussa jaiz tha, Meri Noor. Maine tumhari shart ko halka liya, tumhare jazbaat ko nahi samjha."

Main koi safai nahi de raha. Galti meri thi — not because your shart was big or small, but because the way I refused made you feel unheard. You, who carries every weight quietly, who rarely asks for anything, finally asked for something — and I said no without pausing, without holding space for why you asked.

Tum naraz ho. Tumhe naraz rehne ka poora haq hai. Main manaunga. Not because I want this over fast, but because your anger is yours and I'll sit with it as long as you need me to.
